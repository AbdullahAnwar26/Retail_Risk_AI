**BQ-1 Churn [WORKING DEFINITION - manager to ratify]**
- Population: adult (18+), in-scope jurisdiction, active retail deposit customer with >= 12 months of history (DQ-05). Excluded: minors, age unknown, deceased, bank-initiated closures (fraud/AML/other bank action, as a synthetic flag), and customers with a credit-platform hardship or collections suppression flag.
- Churn event within a **90-day outcome window** after the monthly snapshot date, whichever comes first: (a) **closure**: all retail deposit accounts closed at the customer's request; (b) **salary diversion**: a recurring income credit seen in >= 2 of the 3 months before the snapshot stops for 60 consecutive days while the account stays open; (c) **dormancy**: no customer-initiated transactions for 90 consecutive days.
- Features use only data up to the snapshot date (12-month lookback); labels use only the window after it, so "salary diverted" as a demo driver means a falling or partial income trend before the snapshot, not the label. Evaluation on a time split. Bands low/medium/high on calibrated probability, cut-offs in config.

**BQ-2 Blue-collar worker [WORKING DEFINITION - manager to ratify; PROXY RISK]**
