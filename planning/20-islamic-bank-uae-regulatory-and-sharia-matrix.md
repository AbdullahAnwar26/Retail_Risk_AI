# 20 - Islamic Bank (UAE) Regulatory and Sharia Applicability Matrix (Track B)

**Status:** Planning / research artefact. **Retrieval date for all sources: 2026-11-10.** UAE financial regulation changed substantially in 2025-2026: a new Central Bank law (Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025), a CBUAE AI/ML guidance note (Feb 2026) and a CBUAE Telemarketing Regulation (Mar 2026). Further change is likely and is flagged.
**Scope:** Track B of the two-track design (`21-two-track-deployment-design.md`, DEC-50, DEC-53): the Retail Banking AI Intelligence subsystem (customer and transaction intelligence, segmentation, financial needs, churn, product recommendations, retention insights) deployed **separately** for an Islamic bank. It never makes a credit decision; credit appears only as `CREDIT_REFERRAL` and every output carries `not_for_credit_decisioning` (DEC-42). LLM wording only, and disabled in the MVP (DEC-06, DEC-58). Synthetic data only.
**Working assumption:** Track B = a **UAE onshore, CBUAE-licensed, fully-fledged Islamic bank** [ASSUME - developer/manager to confirm; see §2 for the Islamic-window and DIFC/ADGM variants]. The bank's identity, licence, emirate, size and products are **UNKNOWN**.
**Bank document used for context:** "Retail Credit Policy and Procedures Manual - UAE Islamic Bank v1.0 RC, 9 Nov 2026" (release candidate, **not in force**), cited as "UAE manual ch.X". It is a bank-internal document, not law. Where it is stricter than the sources found, the stricter rule is carried as a bank-policy control (it is tighten-only by its own order of authority, UAE manual ch.1.3).

## 1. Purpose, disclaimer and method

### 1.1 Purpose
- Identify which UAE federal laws, CBUAE regulations, standards and guidance, and which Sharia-governance instruments **may** reach the subsystem's activities in Track B.
- State the condition that decides each one.
- Turn each into **technical design requirements** that the policy layer (file 14, file 21 §4) can enforce whatever legal and Sharia review conclude.
- Produce precise questions for the bank's legal, compliance and Sharia functions (§6).

### 1.2 Disclaimer
> **This is technical planning, NOT legal advice and NOT a Sharia ruling (fatwa).** Every statement about what a law, regulation, standard or Sharia resolution requires, whom it covers, or whether it applies is an interpretation that **requires confirmation by the bank's legal and compliance teams [LEGAL-CONFIRM]** or, for Sharia matters, by **the bank's Internal Sharia Supervision Committee (ISSC) and Sharia Compliance function [SHARIA-CONFIRM]**. That holds even where a row is marked [VERIFIED-SOURCE], which only means the source text was read, not that the reading is correct or complete. The bank's licence, regulator, products and activities are **UNKNOWN**, so every applicability call below is **conditional**. "Likely / Possible / Unlikely / Unknown" is a planning estimate to prioritise design work, not a legal or Sharia conclusion. Nothing here states that a law applies.

### 1.3 Method and labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[VERIFIED-SOURCE]** | The official text or page (centralbank.ae, rulebook.centralbank.ae, u.ae) was opened and read during this session (2026-11-10). The URL is recorded. Only the specific points noted were checked. |
| **[PARTIAL]** | Some points in the row were read at source. Others (named in the row) were not. |
| **[UNVERIFIED]** | From search-result snippets, law-firm or news summaries, or memory; or the source could not be opened. Not to be relied on; copied into §6. |
| **[RECENT]** | Issued, amended or in force in 2025-2026. Check for further change. |
| **[PENDING]** | Draft, consultation or implementing measure not final at retrieval date. |

Source notes:
- **rulebook.centralbank.ae** (the official CBUAE Rulebook) was read as HTML; the "Effective from" and "Status: In-Force" banners were recorded per instrument. Some rulebook URLs returned HTTP 403 to the web-fetch tool but were read through a direct HTTP client.
- **uaelegislation.gov.ae returned HTTP 403** for Federal Decree-Law 45/2021 (PDPL). PDPL points come from u.ae (official portal, read) and secondary sources; they are therefore [PARTIAL] or [UNVERIFIED].
- The CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation and Standards PDFs were downloaded from centralbank.ae and read as extracted text; clause numbers were cross-checked against the Rulebook HTML (Standards listed there as "N 1158/2021, Status: In-Force").
- Neither the CBUAE **Model Management Standards** (MMS) nor any **AAOIFI** Sharia standard text was opened. AAOIFI points are [UNVERIFIED].
- **Arabic is the authoritative language** of UAE federal legislation and of CBUAE instruments issued in both languages [UNVERIFIED as a general rule; LEGAL-CONFIRM]. Only English texts were read.

**Classification of each legal point** (same planning classes as file 13, plus one):

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **BINDING LAW** | Federal law, decree-law, Cabinet resolution or CBUAE regulation/standard with legal force, if the institution is within its scope. |
| **SUPERVISORY GUIDANCE** | CBUAE guidance note or similar. States expectations; by its own terms supplements and does not replace law. |
| **RECOMMENDED CONTROL** | A design or policy choice proposed by this planning work or taken from the bank's own (draft) manual. Not required by the cited source. A bank decision. |
| **UNRESOLVED APPLICABILITY** | The source text is known (or partly known), but whether or how it reaches this bank or this activity is open (licence, free zone, scope exclusion). |
| **SHARIA REQUIREMENT** | A requirement that flows from the Sharia-governance framework (HSA resolutions, AAOIFI standards as adopted, ISSC resolutions). Some are also BINDING LAW because Decree-Law 6/2025 makes HSA resolutions binding (SH01). Content is always [SHARIA-CONFIRM]. |

## 5. Topic cross-walk

Controls are **design requirements**. Whether any is legally or Sharia-required is **[LEGAL-CONFIRM] / [SHARIA-CONFIRM]**.

### 5.1 Data privacy and banking confidentiality
- **Rows:** E01, E02, E05, E09, E12, E13.
- **Controls:** tokenised identifiers, no account numbers (file 08 PRV-02); `CTL-DATA-MINIMISE` (CPR 6.1.1 "minimal amount"); purpose tags; `CTL-DSR-SUPPORT` incl. correction and limiting processing (CPS 6.1.3.6); `CTL-RETENTION` with the CPS 5-year floors (profiling assessments 5.1.1.13; consent copies 6.1.3.4) reconciled with crypto-shredding (§6 IQ10); **staff access logging with name and time**, exportable to the CBUAE on request (CPS 6.1.1); breach hooks (DL 6/2025 Art. 149(3); CPS 6.1.2-6.1.3).

### 5.2 Security, residency and outsourcing (incl. LLM)
- **Rows:** E05, E09, E10, E18.
- **Controls:** `CTL-DATA-RESIDENCY` = `BANK-ON-PREM` in the UAE (cell `B-AE`); Track B shares no data, keys, logs or models with Track A (file 21 TT-2). **LLM:** `llm.hosting ∈ {NONE, BANK_ON_PREM}` (DEC-58); MVP `NONE` (DEC-06). Three independent sources point the same way: CPS 6.1.6.3 (store all consumer and transaction data in the UAE), Outsourcing Reg 6.1/6.3 (Master System of Record in UAE; no sharing abroad without CBUAE approval **and** written customer consent) and AI guidance 2.b (no AI models the LFI has "no control over"); plus the bank rule UAE manual ch.33.2 (customer data never leaves the bank's systems). **A hosted LLM API is a no-go trigger for Track B** (12 §H.4) regardless of the PDPL outcome. Even an on-prem LLM needs: inventory entry and risk rating (E10 2.f, 8.d), tested updates (6.c), kill switch (6.f), and MMS-style governance.

### 5.3 Consent and data use
- **Rows:** E02, E04, E05, E08, E12.
- **Controls:** `CTL-CONSENT-PURPOSE` with `accept_implied: false`; default opted-out for promotion (CPS 5.1.1.9); consent dimensions = purpose x channel x product type x contact method (incl. AI agent) x preferred language (Telemarketing Art. 7); withdrawal propagated within <= 30 calendar days (CPS 6.1.3.6(d)), tighter if configured; DNCR flag = suppress (Telemarketing Art. 9); **no pre-ticked consent** - the subsystem only accepts consent records carrying a positive-action method code (Telemarketing Art. 8 accepts "checking a box"; pre-ticking is not addressed in the sources read; UAE manual ch.18.6/31.1 forbids pre-ticked options and requires express consent for limit increases - limit increases are a credit matter outside our scope, DEC-42).

### 5.4 Profiling and personalised recommendations
- **Rows:** E03, E04, E05, E07, E08, E10, SH03, SH12, SH13.
- **Controls:** written-notice check (customers informed of "Data mined and profiled", CPS 6.1.1.1) as a host-attested precondition for `PROFILE_BUILD`; suitability: recommendations limited to items whose catalogue `target_segments` include the customer's segment (CPS 5.1.1.7) and never unsuitable products (E10 7.d); `CTL-CONTACT-LIMIT` (1/day, 2/week; calls 09:00-18:00); `CTL-CHANNEL-ALLOW` blocks phone for financing referrals (CPS 5.1.1.10); Arabic + English explanations (CPR 2.1.2.8; CPS 2.3.1.3; E10 4.b); `CTL-AI-DISCLOSURE`; 5-year retention of profiling assessment records (CPS 5.1.1.13); Track B overlay controls (SDC-01..SDC-18).

### 5.5 Fairness, discrimination and customer protection
- **Rows:** E02, E04, E06, E07, E10, E14, E17.
- **Controls:** `EXCL-SENSITIVE` for Track B to include **religion, gender, marital/family status, ethnicity/race, nationality** (CPR 10.2.2.4; CPS 10.1.1.4; UAE manual ch.0.6, 5.1 - nationality from the manual), **People-of-Determination status** (protection use only); age only via approved segment definitions [LEGAL-CONFIRM]; `CTL-PROXY-REVIEW` (e.g., residence area, language preference, name-derived features - never used); `CTL-NO-INFERENCE` incl. religion/religiosity (file 21 §4.2); `CTL-FAIRNESS-MONITOR` aggregated by gender, income level, age and marital status (CPS 10.2.1.1), at least yearly and on model change (E10 3.c); `CTL-VULNERABILITY-ROUTE` (CPS 10.1; UAE manual ch.31.4); complaints and human-review path (E06, E10 7.c).

### 5.6 AI/ML governance and explainability
- **Rows:** E10, E11, E19.
- **Controls:** model registry (name, purpose, risk rating, owner, version, approval reference); `CTL-MODEL-APPROVED-ONLY`; annual and on-change bias tests; reason codes + attribution (SHAP or equivalent) in explanations; kill switch per model/module; human-oversight mode recorded per activity (in-the-loop for Mudarabah/investment/Takaful suggestions; on-the-loop for low-risk insights); AI opt-out flag honoured; third-party AI due-diligence pack (if ever any vendor model).

### 5.7 Minors (under 18, and 18-20)
- **Rows:** E04, E08, E15, E16.
- **Controls:** `CTL-MINOR-PROTECT` from `GLOBAL_BASELINE`, **two-tier list unchanged; Tier 1 never relaxed** (file 14 §13.3). For `AE`, `age_of_majority` = 21 [ASSUME, DEC-60], so 18-20-year-olds are routed to the protected journey as well - stricter than CPS 5.1.1.12 (under 18) and consistent with UAE manual ch.1.5 (under-21 out of scope for credit). Telemarketing consent requires age verification (Art. 7). Minors' data excluded from training. MVP: adults only.

### 5.8 Sharia
